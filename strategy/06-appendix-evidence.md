## Open questions the research could not settle

1. **Does the OCR loop perform on camera in real lighting?** (Phase 0, item 1 —
   test before promising.)
2. **Will YouTube surface a new upload to 5-years-dormant subscribers?**
   Unknowable in advance; the Episode-1 ping is a hope, not a plan — the
   strategy works without it.
3. **Can all 1,025 species actually be sourced as physical cards on the
   budget?** Some species may be promo-only or never printed; unresolved — which
   is why "The Impossible Last Cards" exists as an episode engine and why the
   career-catch/Community-Dex counters run alongside the 1025 counter.
4. **PokéAPI longevity** — cache from day one.

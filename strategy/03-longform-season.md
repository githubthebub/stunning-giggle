# 03 — Long-Form & Season 1: "The Bulk-Bin Dex"

Long-form is where the funnel lands (Shorts convert to subs at only ~0.05–0.5%;
the Related-Video click into a weekly episode is where viewers become an
audience), where the 4,000 watch-hours for monetization accrue (Shorts-feed time
doesn't count), and where the story lives.

## The season premise

**Register every one of the 1,025 species from real, physical cards — on a
bulk-bin budget — then give the entire physical collection away.**

The budget is the show's constraint engine. Frame it with the cleaner
headline number: **complete the entire dex for
1/10,000th of what his one card sold for** (~$1,650 ceiling), with a stretch
goal the season chases in public: *can the core be done for under $100?*
Bulk lots are assumed at ~$20–30 per 1,000 cards (an assumption, not a verified
price — Phase 0 price-checks it before Episode 1 scripts it); the real question — how many *unique
species* a bulk-bin budget can reach — is genuinely unknown, which makes it
tension, not a lie. The season is honest about its own arithmetic on camera.

**Two counters run at all times** (this is the pivot-proofing — the design's
one non-negotiable):

1. **The Dex: x/1025** — the quest counter (dies if the app ever dies).
2. **Career catches + the Community Dex %** — cumulative counters that survive
   any pivot, because they count *acts of catching*, not Nintendo's species list.

If a C&D ever lands, counter #2 and the characters carry the season across the
asset swap ([05-legal-survival.md](05-legal-survival.md)). A season with one
counter is a hostage; a season with two is a story with a subplot.

## The character layer

- **The Dex** — sarcastic, weary, seen-it-all narrator (your Starter Squad
  voice, formalized). Introduced by video 3. The Dex *is* the channel's brand;
  it is also original IP that no takedown touches.
- **The lore seed (plant in Episode 2, pay off whenever needed):** the Dex
  occasionally *glitches* — flickers, misreads, hints that it can see creatures
  "the license doesn't cover." This foreshadows the original-creature pack as a
  story beat the audience anticipates, instead of a legal press release they
  mourn.

## The Entralink B-plot (the participation engine, correctly sequenced)

The game's serverless battle codes are a gift: **every episode ends with the
episode's best catches encoded as a battle link in the pinned comment.** Viewers
fight the episode's actual team; the weirdest winning team (Von Restorff: feature
the weird, not the strong) gets featured next episode, and a weekly leaderboard
lives in the pinned thread.

**Sequencing discipline (non-negotiable):** participation
formats *amplify* an audience; they cannot *bootstrap* one. The battle links are
a B-plot garnish from day one but never load-bearing until the audience exists
(~5k engaged subs). No hook may ever depend on a participation number you can't
guarantee. The main pipeline — scan, ceremony, verdict — must always be filmable
alone at a kitchen table, exactly as entertaining at 200 views as at 2M.

## Milestone unlocks (goal-gradient)

Public, pre-announced, app-roadmap unlocks tied to subscriber milestones:
- **2,500 subs:** Shiny-odds display in the scanner UI
- **10,000 subs:** the Community Dex page goes live (aggregate % of all 1,025
  registered across all players)
- **25,000 subs:** the Dex's "glitch" resolves — the original-creature pack
  ships as a season event

The audience's growth becomes co-owned progress — and the pivot pack's release
is now a *reward*, not a retreat.

## Shorts ↔ long-form plumbing

- Every short is an atomic moment cut from a long-form scan session — **one
  shoot, both formats.**
- Every short's Related Video link and pinned comment point at the *current*
  episode (weekly cadence keeps the target fresh; a monthly cadence would leave
  the funnel pointing at stale inventory for weeks).
- Cliffhanger shorts withhold the rare-catch payoff to the episode
  ("card #999 took three months — the full story →").
- Episode cold-opens replay the week's best short moment in the first 15
  seconds, so funnel arrivals land on familiar ground.

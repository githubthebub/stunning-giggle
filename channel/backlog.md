### 6. This Game Erases a Type That Doesn't Exist Yet

**Thumbnail:** Togepi mid-crossing on the white bridge, its pink Fairy type-badge dissolving into plain gray Normal. Overlay: **"TYPE ERASED"**

**Format:** lore-doc

**Hook:** "Watch this Togepi cross from the Dream World into Unova. The instant it arrives, its type is gone — rewritten to Normal, because this world has no word for what it was."

**Premise:** Episode 1's Layer 3 expanded into a full border-forensics documentary. The Dream World's pool really contains Fairy-types — Togepi, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Marill, Cottonee — and the game's own crossover code really rewrites Fairy to Normal on arrival, with the comment in the source spelling out why: Gen 5 Unova has no Fairy type. **Ladder:** Turns the flagship's fastest beat into standalone, reproducible evidence, and hands the series its scariest recurring rule — crossing over changes you — which pays off in videos #7 and #10.

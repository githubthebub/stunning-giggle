# 05 — The 90-Day Sprint (start today)

Eight years is won or lost in the first 90 days — not because of the subs
you'll gain (they'll be modest) but because this sprint installs the machine:
niche locked, Idea Engine running, 12 packaging-first videos shipped, weekly
review habit set. Do the sprint, and Phase 0 is simply "keep going."

## Week 1 — Pick the battlefield (niche selection)

Score every niche you're considering 1–10 on four axes; multiply the scores:

| Axis | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Obsession** | Could I make 300 videos about this without dying inside? (You'll need to.) |
| **Proof of demand** | Are there existing channels with outlier videos pulling 1M+ views? (No outliers = no wave.) |
| **Room on the wave** | Is the format frontier still moving, or is it locked up by 3 giants? (Rising categories > mature ones.) |
| **Unfair advantage** | Do I have skill, access, story, or resources most people in this niche don't? |

- The multiplied score makes weakness expensive: a 9-9-9-**2** niche (loves
  it, big demand, open field, zero edge) scores 1,458 and loses to a
  7-7-7-**7** (2,401). One dead axis kills the niche.
- **Format-wave candidates to research this week** (mid-2026 frontier —
  verify with your own outlier research, don't trust any list including this
  one): AI-augmented challenge formats, real-world competition in
  under-served regions, education-as-spectacle ("learning X in 30 days"
  ladders), hyper-niche sports/skills going mainstream, "creator builds real
  business/thing" durational formats.
- **Deliverable by Sunday:** one niche, written down, with its scores and
  your one-sentence unfair advantage.

## Week 2 — Build the intelligence base

- Create the [idea bank](templates/idea-bank.md). Populate it with **50
  outlier videos** from 30+ channels (see
  [02 — the Idea Engine](02-the-idea-engine.md), Step 1).
- Write down the **3 format skeletons** behind those outliers.
- Generate your first **50 video ideas** via the remix formula. Score all 50
  on "would a stranger click?" Keep the 8+s.
- **Deliverable:** idea bank with 50 outliers + 50 scored ideas, top 12
  flagged.

## Weeks 3–13 — Ship 12 videos (one per week, zero exceptions)

Weekly loop, in order:

1. **Mon — Package:** pick the idea, write 10 titles, mock 3 thumbnails, fill
   the [video scorecard](templates/video-scorecard.md). Scorecard fails →
   next idea. Never film unpackaged.
2. **Tue–Wed — Produce:** film the promise. Cold-open, stakes by 0:15, open
   loop by 0:30 (see [03](03-retention-and-format.md)).
3. **Thu–Fri — Post:** edit ruthlessly — every 60–90s needs a payoff, raise,
   or turn. Cut the rest. Also cut 3 Shorts from the footage.
4. **Sat — Publish** long-form (consistent day/time). Shorts spread across
   the week.
5. **Sun — Review:** 30 minutes, [weekly review](templates/weekly-review.md).
   CTR vs retention diagnosis, one lesson, one change for next week.

Format testing cadence inside the 12: videos 1–4 = format candidate A,
videos 5–8 = candidate B, videos 9–12 = candidate C **unless** A or B produced
a ≥5x-median outlier — in that case, drop everything and escalate that format
for all remaining weeks (see the discovery protocol in
[03](03-retention-and-format.md)).

## Day 90 — The gate

Sit down with the idea bank, 13 weekly reviews, and the analytics. Answer
three questions:

1. **Did any video 3x+ my median?** → That format is your Phase 0 lead. Next
   90 days: escalate it.
2. **Is CTR trending up across the 12?** → Packaging skill is compounding.
   Stay the course even if absolute views are small.
3. **Did I ship 12 of 12?** → If yes, you're already executing better than
   ~95% of channels, and the 8-year clock is officially running.
   If no — the problem isn't the niche, the format, or the algorithm. Fix the
   production system before touching the strategy.

Then re-read [01 — the Master Plan](01-the-master-plan.md) and run the next
90 days. It's 90-day sprints all the way up — roughly 32 of them to the crown.

## The three rules that outrank everything else in this folder

1. **The idea is 80% of the video.** A great video of a bad idea gets no views.
   A decent video of a great idea goes viral. Spend your effort accordingly.
2. **Title and thumbnail are decided before you film — or you don't film.**
   If you wouldn't click it, nobody else will.
3. **You don't beat MrBeast at MrBeast.** Every #1 in history rode a format
   wave he invented or got to first. Your job is to own the *next* wave, not
   to re-run his.

## The scoreboard (know what "bigger" means)

- **Bigger by views/month** — winnable in ~4–5 years. Channels have out-viewed
  MrBeast in individual months before. This is your first crown.
- **Bigger by cultural relevance in your category** — winnable in ~3 years.
  Be the person a category is synonymous with.
- **Bigger by subscribers** — the full 8-year campaign. Requires winning the
  first two crowns and then going global (Phase 3–4).

# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: placeholder date_

Compass is built around a simple promise: your reflections belong to you, and they stay on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means in practice.

## The short version

- Everything you create in Compass — reflections, journal entries, mood check-ins, and answers to prompts — is stored **only on your device**.
- There is **no account** to create. We never ask for your name, email, or password.
- There is **no backend, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics SDK**.
- The app **does not transmit your personal content over the network**. Your words never leave your phone unless you deliberately export or share them yourself.

## What we collect

Nothing leaves your device by default. Compass does not send your reflections, entries, moods, or answers to us or to anyone else, because there is no "us" on the other end of a network connection — there is no server that receives your content.

The only data Compass works with is the content you enter, and it lives in local storage on your device.

## Where your data is stored

All of your content is kept in the app's local storage on your device. It is **encrypted at rest** using the encryption facilities provided by your device's operating system, so that a stored file cannot be casually read outside the app.

Because your data lives only on your device:

- **Uninstalling the app deletes your data.** Removing Compass, or clearing its data through your device settings, erases the local content.
- **There is no server copy.** We do not hold a backup, so there is nothing on a server for us to delete and no server-side deletion request to make. Deleting locally is the whole story.
- **You are in control of exports.** If you choose to export or share an entry, that action is initiated by you and leaves the app under your direction. Once content leaves the app this way, this policy no longer governs it.

## Things outside the app's control

Your device's operating system, app store, and any crash-reporting built into the platform may collect their own diagnostic or crash data according to their own policies. That is a function of the OS and store you use, not of Compass, and it is outside the app's control. Compass itself does not attach your personal content to any such data — no reflections, entries, moods, or answers are sent by the app.

If Compass ever adds its own telemetry in the future, it will be **strictly opt-in** and **content-free**: it would never include the words you write, and it would be off unless you explicitly turn it on.

## Children and age

Compass is intended for adults and is configured for users **18 and older**. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app stores data only on the device and transmits no personal content, there is no server-side collection of any user's information, regardless of age.

## Questions

Because there is no account and no server, most privacy questions come down to your own device: what you keep, what you export, and what you delete. You remain in control of all of it.

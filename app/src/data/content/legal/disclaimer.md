# Before You Begin

Welcome to Compass. Take a moment to read this — it matters.

Compass is a self-reflection and coaching tool. It's here to help you think more clearly, notice your patterns, and move toward what matters to you. That's it. That's the whole job.

## What Compass Is Not

Compass is **not** therapy, and it is **not** medical or mental-health care. It is not a substitute for professional help of any kind. It does not diagnose anything, and it does not treat anything. There is no one on the other side of the screen — Compass offers curated prompts and reflections, not personalized care.

Think of it as a well-designed mirror and a pushy-but-caring notebook. Useful for reflection. Not a replacement for a real relationship with a qualified professional.

## When to Reach Out to Someone

Reflection has limits, and honesty about those limits is part of taking yourself seriously.

If something in your life feels heavier than a journaling prompt can hold — if you're struggling in a way that keeps coming back, or affecting your sleep, work, or relationships — please talk to a qualified mental-health professional. Reaching out is a strength, not a failure. You can also lean on the people you trust: a friend, a family member, a doctor, someone who knows you.

**If you are in crisis or worried about your safety or anyone else's, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your area right now.** Compass is not built for emergencies, and you deserve real, immediate support from a person who can help.

## Your Data Stays With You

Everything you write and reflect on in Compass stays on your device. Your thoughts are yours.

## Ready?

Use Compass as a place to be honest with yourself — and let it point you toward the real people and support that can carry what an app cannot.

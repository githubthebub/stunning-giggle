# Upload Checklist & Channel Ops

## Cadence

- **1 long-form episode / week** — same day, same hour (consistency is a
  ranking input *and* a ritual: "the restoration finishes at 8:14 PM Friday").
  Premiere it at 8:00 PM; end the premiere chat at 8:14. Yes, really.
- **3 Shorts / week** — each one either compresses last week's entry or teases
  the next. Every Short ends on "Entry restored. N of 151." + channel pointer.
- **1 community post / week** — the current state of the corrupted photo as an
  image. Zero effort, maximum theory-posting.

## Tags (rotate, don't dump)

pokemon fanfic · pokemon lore · pokemon theory · lost pokedex · pokemon
creepypasta (only on dread episodes) · pokemon story · [featured Pokémon name]
· pokemon iceberg · trainer id 000

## First-hour protocol (the algorithm's first impression)

1. Pin the comment-bait question **before** the video is public.
2. Reply to the first 20 substantive comments — replies double comment counts.
3. Heart theory comments; never confirm or deny a theory. Ever. Season-long
   ambiguity is the product.
4. Post the episode's clip-line quote as a community post at hour 2.

## Pinned comment bank

- Ep 1: "Team League Cap 🔴 or Team Pokéball ⬜? Wrong answers only."
- Ep 2: "Transcribe what you hear in the last 3 seconds of the recording. Everyone hears it differently. That's not a bug."
- Shorts: one question, ≤15 words, answerable by a 10-year-old and debatable by a 30-year-old.

## Retention review (after every upload)

- Check audience-retention graph at the pattern-break timestamps in the script.
  A dip >8% at a break = that break type is wearing out; rotate it.
- If intro (0:00–0:20) retention < 70%, the *next* episode's cold open gets
  shorter, not louder.

## Legal & safety rails

- Fan fiction narration over **original art / commissioned art / licensed
  assets only** — no game sprites, no anime footage, no official artwork, no
  extracted audio. This is what keeps fanfic channels monetizable and
  strike-free.
- Disclaimer in every description (template above) and in channel About.
- Tone rail: PG-13. Grief yes, gore no. If an entry needs a content note,
  put it on screen at 0:00 like the pros do.
- Don't use "Pokémon" as the *first* word of the channel name; "The Lost
  Pokédex — Fan Stories" keeps the fan-work status unambiguous.

## Season 1 success metrics

- North star: **% of viewers who watch 2+ episodes** (serialization is the bet).
- Shorts→subscriber conversion > 2% means the funnel works; below 1%, the
  Shorts are entertaining strangers instead of recruiting viewers — retune the
  final 10 seconds, not the hook.
- The finale ("Entry 151") should be scheduled only after episode 6 doubles the
  baseline — mid-season bomb is the growth inflection; protect it.

# Titles & Thumbnails — A/B Kit

Rules this kit follows:

- **Title states the impossible thing plainly.** No clickbait punctuation storms;
  the premise *is* the bait. First person outperforms third person in this niche.
- **Thumbnail = one object + ≤3 words.** The cracked dex is the channel's face.
  No arrows, no shocked-face insets, no red circles.
- **Never spoil the twist.** Titles sell the question, the video sells the answer.
- Run A/B via YouTube's "Test & compare" on every upload; kill the loser fast.

## Evergreen title formulas for the series

- "I restored Entry [N]. I wish I hadn't." *(dread episodes)*
- "The official dex says [canon line]. Mine says [4-word contradiction]."
- "[Pokémon] wasn't [canon behavior]. It was waiting."
- "The photo is [X]% restored. You can see [object] now."

## Thumbnail system (make once, reuse forever)

- **Base plate:** the cracked dex on the desk, lamp light, 20% vignette.
- **Variable layer:** whatever the dex screen shows (entry name, file size,
  photo corruption %). Episode-to-episode consistency turns the feed into a
  serialized poster wall — binge-signal for the algorithm and for humans.
- **Photo-restoration meter** in the corner of every episode thumbnail
  (▓▓░░░░ 12%). It makes the *thumbnail itself* a cliffhanger.

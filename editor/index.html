🪶 How FeatherCut stays off your storage & RAM

Nothing is installed. This whole editor is one small web page. Desktop editors like Premiere Pro install 4 GB+ and idle at multiple GB of RAM — FeatherCut exists only while this tab is open, and closing the tab frees everything instantly.

  • Imports use zero storage. Your files are linked straight from disk (never copied, never uploaded anywhere). The browser streams small chunks as needed.
  • Exports stream to disk. In Chrome/Edge, the export is written to your chosen file as it renders, so even long videos don't pile up in memory.
  • Everything stays private. There is no server and no account — your footage never leaves your computer.

Tips: use 720p while editing and switch to 1080p for the final export; close other heavy tabs while exporting; export runs in real time (a 2-minute video takes 2 minutes) — keep the tab visible while it runs.
